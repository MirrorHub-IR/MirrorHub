# Community Code of Conduct

MirrorHub aims to be a constructive, respectful, and inclusive open-source project. Participation in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces is covered by this Code of Conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions, not the character or identity of contributors.
- Give actionable feedback and explain technical reasoning.
- Welcome questions from people with different experience levels.
- Accept good-faith disagreement and focus on the project's goals.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and relevant security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, humiliation, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam, impersonation, or knowingly misleading project participants.
- Unwanted sexual attention or content.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy. Depending on severity and context, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a participant from project spaces. Security reports should be handled privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting a concern

For a concern involving a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not post exploit details publicly. For other conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the contact route published on the [MirrorHub organization profile](https://github.com/MirrorHub-IR). Include relevant links and context, but do not disclose unrelated personal information.

Reports will be reviewed as carefully and confidentially as the project resources allow. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies within the repository and in project spaces where someone is representing MirrorHub or its community.
